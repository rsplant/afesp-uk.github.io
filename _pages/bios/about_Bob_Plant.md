I'm Bob Plant, a professor at the Department of Meteorology and the University of Reading. My role within AFESP is as PhD supervisor for [Kaustubh Mittal]({% link _pages/bios/about_Kaustubh_Mittal.md %}) and [Ken Rui Fong]({% link _pages/bios/about_Ken_Rui_Fong.md %}), working on medium-range forecast busts and on the prospects for multi-scale parameterization. 

More broadly, I am interested in moist convection and boundary layer turbulence, particularly their effects on mesoscale and larger-scale flows and their appropriate representation in models at various scales. To see more about my work in these areas, you can look at [my webpage]({% link https://www.met.reading.ac.uk/~sws00rsp/home/ %})
